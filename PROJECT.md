# PebbleDetective app for Android

We are designing and building an Android app for kids.

It allows:

  - to open a live view with the frontal camera of the phone to capture the image of a pebble or a rock that kid found;
  - it has a cool looking dynamic focusing widget overlay that allows selecting the rock in the live view by tapping on the screen to point out the pebble center, once the view is stabilised and tapping has happened make a "target captured" snapshot and save the picture, making it still from that point;
  - it then offers a cool dynamic pop-up that asks whether to do a "Deep Research" where upon pressing "Yes" the screen goes into a matrix-like cool "thinking mode" for about five seconds with a stylish progress bar at the bottom of the screen, at the start of the research show smth that suggest we are connecting to a satellite for a signal;
  - it then shows a picture of one of the planets of our solar system or sun itself (chosen based on the dominant color of the pebble, but for gray or unknown color pebbles just pick the planet at random) with the name of the planet displayed;
  - it then offers an option to see a cool 3D schematic simulation of a schematic pebble flying from the given planet to Earth through space (the simulation takes 10 to 15 seconds end to end and includes meaningful sounds);
  - the app supports three languages and the language can be toggled at any stage across three languages;
  - the app has cool meaningful sound effects for each stage of the workflow/gameplay, where the sound can be switched on and off (and is off by default);
  - the app keeps the record and pictures of all pebbles researched (save the log with date, time, location and file to disk, make it persistent and available for retrospective research). The static picture is taken during the research phase.
